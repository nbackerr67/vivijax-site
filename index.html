About ViviJax

ViviJax Media Group is an independent company based in Texas. We build practical software and publish content for people who want to live, work, and grow with more intention. Our focus is on simple, useful products that respect people's time and privacy.

What We Do

Mobile Apps

We design and develop apps for iPhone, built to be simple, focused, and respectful of your data. Our first app is currently in development.

Publishing

We publish books and written works, from first draft through print and digital release.

Digital Content

We create practical content and resources that help people apply what they learn to real life.

Our Approach

  • Practical first. Every product should be useful on day one.
  • Privacy respected. We aim to collect only what a product needs to work.
  • Steady progress. We improve our products in small, regular releases.
  • Built with integrity. We keep our commitments to the people who use our work.
